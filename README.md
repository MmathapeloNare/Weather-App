A clean, responsive weather app built with React. Search for any city to see the current conditions, an hourly forecast for today and the outlook for the days ahead. The background changes with the local time of the city you search, from sunrise to day, sunset and night.
Features.
City search: type any city and get its weather (defaults to Johannesburg)
Current conditions: temperature, today's high and low, "feels like", the condition and its icon
Plain-language details: wind, humidity and UV index, each with a short description such as "Light breeze", "Comfortable" or "High"
Hourly forecast: temperature, icon and chance of rain for every hour of today
Daily forecast: the next days with the high and low, condition and chance of rain
Time-aware background: the colour scheme changes between sunrise, day, sunset and night based on the searched city's local time
Loading and error messages: clear feedback while loading and when a city isn't found

Built with;
Frontend.

-React
-Vite
-date-fns for date and time formatting
-CSS

Backend.

-Node.js and Express
-WeatherAPI.com for the weather data
